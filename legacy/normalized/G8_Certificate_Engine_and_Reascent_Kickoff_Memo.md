G8 Certificate Engine and Re-Ascent Programme Memo
Consolidate the exact solver/proof compiler, recover the frozen scale frontier, then climb again
11 August 2026
| PROJECT TYPE | NEXT PROJECT — STAGED ENGINEERING + SCIENTIFIC RE-ASCENT |
| --- | --- |
G7 answered the scientific question “does the typed proof architecture climb?” positively. G8 should therefore not spend a whole programme collecting more nearby symbolic wins. Its mission is to close the execution/certificate gap that stopped prospectively selected high-branch and seven-man arenas, compile the G7 proof language into repository-quality certificates, and then use that stronger engine to resume prospective ascent.
# 1. Starting evidence from G7
- Exact higher-tree successes: F01/F02 six-man bishop races and F09 higher-material faithful fortress.
- Destination composition scales: material-endpoint Branch/Hyperkernel gives exact two-way and irreducible three-way certificates.
- New strategy-language layer: FILTERED_ATTRACTOR + universal FILTER_PIVOT factorization, held-out and backward exact.
- Proof DAGs can be nested: lower exact endpoints → Hyperkernel → derived target → second-order attractor → strategy-language factorization.
- Current engineering frontier: prospectively selected F03/F04/F06/F07/F08 do not deliver full W/D/L under the monolithic exact engine; F04/F06 exceed 180 s despite clean edge audits.
- Logical certificate seeds are already compact relative to enumerated tables; current byte-per-state masks and repeated move generation are engineering overhead, not proof-language necessity.
# 2. G8 north-star questions
1. Can a material-signature dependency DAG solver recover the frozen G7 scale holds without changing their prospective arena definitions?
1. Can proof objects be compiled into compact, independently verifiable certificates rather than research-process bit masks?
1. Can exact solve/runtime scale substantially better when lower-material signatures are solved once, packed and reused rather than materialized inside one optional-material arena?
1. Can FILTERED_ATTRACTOR/FILTER_PIVOT be generalized from a single CHECK language to a frozen algebra of semantic move languages without overfitting?
1. Can destination composition and strategy-language composition be compiled as a product proof DAG with deterministic dependency/version semantics?
1. At what point do proof certificates allow useful exact ascent even when complete W/D/L enumeration becomes expensive?
1. Can the upgraded engine prospectively certify at least one previously held seven-man/high-branch family and one new materially higher family?
1. Can the verifier reproduce G6/G7 frozen checksums independently enough to make the programme repository-quality rather than session-quality?
# 3. Hard design rules
- The frozen G7 hold arenas F03/F04/F06/F07/F08 must be attempted unchanged first. Do not shrink or alter them because the new engine still struggles.
- No outcome-guided tuning of indexing, symmetry or material decomposition may change game semantics. Engineering may change representation, not the arena.
- Promotion, capture and lower-material transitions must be typed as explicit dependency edges. No shortcut promotion model may silently replace faithful continuation where the arena requires it.
- Every compact certificate must be checked by an independent verifier path that does not merely replay the producer’s internal queue state.
- Strategy-language palettes must be predeclared semantically before inspecting which filters compress a target best. Avoid learning absolute-square move filters.
- Preserve the two frozen G6 historical exceptions as provenance-only unless new implementation artifacts actually appear.
- If a held arena still fails after the intended engine redesign, freeze the new measured boundary rather than substituting an easier truth-guided slice.

# 5. Strategy-language algebra to test prospectively
G8 should broaden the FILTERED_ATTRACTOR idea, but under a predeclared finite palette rather than arena-specific search. Suggested semantic languages are hypotheses, not assumptions of usefulness:
- CHECK — frozen G7 baseline.
- CAPTURE — attacker progress constrained to captures before target entry.
- FORCING = CHECK ∪ CAPTURE — broad tactical language.
- KING_ONLY / PAWN_ONLY / MOBILE_PIECE_ONLY — resource-specific handoff languages.
- CHECK_OR_PROMOTION_THREAT — only if “promotion threat” is given an exact move-local semantic definition before outcome inspection.
- Finite unions/intersections of the frozen palette, with description cost charged explicitly.
The scientific target is not to find the filter with the largest coverage. It is to learn whether strategy languages form reusable nested or switching structures, whether multiple Filter-Pivot layers compose, and whether strategy-language certificates remain compact under ascent.

# 9. Failure criteria
- The new engine changes arena semantics or terminal models to obtain a speedup.
- Most speed gains come only from shrinking/restricting the prospectively frozen G7 hold arenas.
- Producer and verifier share so much hidden state that “independent verification” is effectively queue replay.
- Certificate size approaches full truth-table size before reusable composition appears.
- Strategy-language search degenerates into square IDs or post-hoc move predicates chosen from W/D/L.
- Recovered scale comes only from parallel brute force while material-signature decomposition/proof reuse adds negligible value.
- The programme spends most of G8 re-proving already frozen G6/G7 science and never re-ascends after engineering consolidation.
- High-branch/seven-man holds remain unsolved and the new engine produces no sharper causal scaling diagnosis.
# 10. Stopping rules
- Stop optimizing a representation once its cost is no longer material to recovering a frozen hold or shrinking certificate economics.
- Stop adding strategy filters after the predeclared palette unless a new semantic language is motivated by an independently observed mechanism and then prospectively held out before evaluation.
- Stop a new ascent family if it exceeds the revised certificate budget without changing the transition/scaling question; preserve the boundary.
- Do not postpone re-ascent indefinitely in pursuit of a perfect engine. G8 must include at least one genuinely new prospective higher-tree experiment after the core recovery campaign.
- Do not repair the two frozen G6 historical certification exceptions unless new original artifacts become available.
# 11. Ambitious G8 end-state
A successful G8 should leave the programme with something qualitatively different from another collection of solved slices: a reusable exact material-signature solver, a compact proof-object compiler, an independent verifier, recovered high-branch/seven-man G7 holds, a strategy-language algebra layered on the destination calculus, and at least one new prospective ascent result produced by that infrastructure. The strongest positive result would be a seven-man or similarly hostile arena whose full exact truth is recovered through signature decomposition while a much smaller mixed proof DAG independently certifies a large outcome region. The equally valuable negative would be a reproducible frontier showing that even compact proof verification approaches enumeration cost beyond a specific branching/dependency regime.
