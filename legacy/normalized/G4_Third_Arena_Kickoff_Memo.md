# G4 Kickoff Memo
Third-arena replication of exact compositional proof mechanisms
NEXT EXPERIMENT - TEST CROSS-ARENA REUSE; DO NOT OPTIMIZE FOR A PREDETERMINED POSITIVE RESULT
10 August 2026
# Mission
G4 tests whether the proof architecture established by Pilots 1-2 and G3 survives a materially different arena. The target is not another accurate classifier. The experiment must determine whether exact lower-material inheritance, semantic certified targets and strict predicate-to-predicate reachability remain useful when the movement topology changes.
# 1. Starting evidence
Pilot 1 showed hierarchical compression and a real mobile-king complexity warning. Pilot 2 independently reproduced exact composition in K+2P vs K: KPK inheritance plus a staggered-pawn target certified 97.69% of wins. G3 added strict reachability back into the union of existing predicates, raising exact coverage to 98.94%, while the final 214 mixed blockade geometries remained near lookup-scale under a bounded local-access DSL.
# 2. North-star question
Can a materially different exact chess arena reuse the same proof architecture - lower-material projection, semantic certified targets and strict adversarial bridges between targets - so that symbolic coverage grows faster than description cost?
# 3. Recommended arena
Primary recommendation: a deliberately restricted K+B+P vs K+N arena. The knight introduces local non-linear control, the bishop a long-range colour-complex manifold, and the pawn promotion/capture transitions. This is a stronger independence test than another pure-pawn arena.
Choose restrictions for exact tractability and clean symmetry, not to bake in an expected theorem. A fixed central or near-central pawn file and fixed bishop colour complex are reasonable starting constraints. Declare all restrictions before solving and hold them fixed.
# 4. Fallback arena
If K+B+P vs K+N cannot be made certificate-quality at manageable scale, use a predeclared restricted K+2P vs K+P family. It offers strong lower-material inheritance and interacting promotion races but is a weaker independence test, so the reason for switching must be documented.
# 5. Required phases
## A. Freeze and certify the arena
Declare state encoding, static validity, legal moves, captures, promotion, checkmate/stalemate semantics, side to move and symmetries. Enumerate raw and static-valid universes.
## B. Exact baseline solve
Compute exact outcome truth and layer/rank histograms. Use exact finite-game truth, not engine evaluation.
## C. Lower-material projection sweep
Solve required lower-material subgames exactly. Test restoration/inheritance implications and classify every counterexample.
## D. Dimensional collapse
Group out candidate piece coordinates and measure universal-win, mixed and universal-draw geometries.
## E. Semantic predicate discovery
Search race, movement-manifold, contact, support, interception and formation atoms. Promote only zero-counterexample rules.
## F. Strict stepping-stone tests
Terminalize exact predicates and compute adversarial reachability. Treat shortcuts explicitly.
## G. Predicate-union bridge
Once two mechanisms exist, terminalize their union and test whether additional residual states can force return to the certified library.
## H. Residual grammar
Only then attack the critical manifold with a local DSL. Stop if exact description approaches lookup scale.
# 6. First-class proof operations
- PROJECT: map a state to exact lower-material subgames after legal material deletion/reduction.
- INHERITS: test a finite implication under restoration of removed material and enumerate exceptions.
- TARGET: terminalize an exact semantic predicate.
- ATTRACTOR: compute exact exists/forall adversarial predecessor closure.
- BRIDGE: compute strict reachability to a union of certified predicates.
- RESIDUAL: subtract all certified layers.
- GROUP_OUT: audit universal/mixed outcome classes after removing a piece coordinate.
- CERTIFY: emit zero-counterexample scans and deterministic artifacts/hashes.

# 8. G4 advance criteria
- At least one useful exact lower-material inheritance rule exists, or failures form a small semantic exception family.
- At least one arena-specific semantic predicate certifies a nontrivial exact region.
- At least one predicate works as a strict adversarial reachability target.
- The union of certified predicates attracts additional residual states, replicating the G3 bridge operation.
- Combined proof coverage grows materially faster than predicate description cost.
- The unresolved region localizes to a lower-dimensional movement/contact manifold, even if its final grammar is complex.
- At least one proof operation or predicate template is genuinely reused from earlier pilots without square-level special casing.
# 9. Warning / failure criteria
- Lower-material projections provide no reusable implication and counterexamples are heterogeneous.
- Exact predicates explode into square-specific exception catalogues from the outset.
- Strict target reachability adds little beyond direct membership.
- Predicate-union bridging adds no meaningful coverage.
- The residual remains high-dimensional rather than localizing.
- Description cost tracks raw state growth before a final critical boundary is reached.
- Apparent reuse depends on tailoring the arena to earlier geometry.
# 10. Hard methodological rules
- Do not use Stockfish evaluation as proof truth.
- Do not change terminal semantics after seeing results without versioning and rerunning all downstream claims.
- Do not count classifier accuracy as proof.
- Flatten learned rules and exhaustively verify them before theorem status.
- Do not count promotion/capture/material-reduction shortcuts as target success unless the target explicitly includes them.
- Enumerate checkmate, stalemate and no-move anomalies rather than discarding them.
- Stop when the predeclared description-cost boundary is crossed.
- Persist enough deterministic artifacts to reproduce every exact claim.

First build a generic finite-game proof harness implementing the operations above. Re-run the Pilot 2 G3 union bridge as a regression test. The harness should reproduce 2,852 bridge wins and 98.94% combined coverage before any third-arena rules are trusted.
