# 2. G3: attack the 214-geometry blockade frontier with the right DSL
Do not continue fitting flat trees to absolute squares. The evidence says the remaining variable is local access topology around BK and the two pawns. The next representation should make that topology primitive.
- Pawn-pair frame: advanced/rear pawn, rank gap, leader file, support relation.
- BK blockade frame: corridor distance, rank relative to each pawn, front/side/rear sector.
- Critical support squares: squares from which WK protects a push, controls a capture square, or establishes opposition.
- King-access regions: reachability to a critical support set while treating BK and pawn occupancy as moving/conditional obstacles.
- Tempo-aware opposition atoms: side to move plus parity of king routes to contact squares.
- Local transformations: mirror reflection and pawn-order canonicalization.
- Small unions of contact regions rather than raw 64-square masks.
Success should be measured by exact description length and template reuse, not generic classification accuracy.

# 7. Make lower-material inheritance a first-class solver operation
Pilot 2's strongest result came from reusing exact KPK truth. Future solvers should automatically project a state onto material-reduction subgames and ask whether a solved lower-material predicate is monotone under restoration of the removed piece, with explicit detection of exceptions such as stalemate.
1. Enumerate legal material-deletion projections.
1. Query exact lower-material W/D/L predicates.
1. Test inheritance as a finite implication over the larger arena.
1. Classify every counterexample by terminal mechanism or strategic interference.

# 8. General symbolic predicate DSL after two pilots
- Distance and tempo atoms: king distances, pawn moves to promotion, parity and move order.
- Material-projection atoms: lower-material W/D/L membership.
- Formation atoms: pawn rank gap, adjacency, protected passer, connected pawns.
- Region atoms: corridor, front/side/rear blockade sectors, king-access regions.
- Movement-manifold atoms: bishop diagonal, rook line, knight neighbourhood, pawn corridor.
- Projection/contact operations: nearest point, support square, capture square, promotion square.
- Interval/region constructors and small parameterized unions.
- Exact reachability-to-predicate operators, so a predicate can become a certified stepping stone rather than only a classifier.
