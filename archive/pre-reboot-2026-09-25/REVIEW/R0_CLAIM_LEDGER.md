## R0 interpretation rule

This ledger reconstructs what the repository actually supports at proposition level. An R0 status of `SUPPORTED_AT_STATED_SCOPE` means the surviving sources coherently support the stated proposition and scope; it is **not** a fresh independent rerun or publication-grade certification unless the verifier field explicitly says so. `NEEDS_DEEP_AUDIT` marks mathematically or computationally load-bearing claims whose full proof/code/artifact replay should be a later review target. `DEPENDENCY_BROKEN` is used where provenance/replay evidence does not support the historical authority level.

The ledger deliberately separates solved-state scale, discovery leverage, certificate compression, and global connectivity. A large table is not a start-position connection; a small certificate is not evidence that truth was cheap to discover.

## R0 synthesis across claim classes

### A. Solved-state scale
The strongest reported exact/replayed scale is G8 R01/R02, roughly 82.5–88.4 million dependency states and 3.9–4.4 million full-material states, but these are highly restricted eight-man arenas with fixed pawn placements and historical restricted state semantics. G12 is much smaller but is the relevant full-rule anchor: clean-root KQK/KRK/KPK plus an exact DEAD family under G10 semantics/G12 serialization.

### B. Discovery leverage
Strict attractor, Branch/Hyperkernel and Filter-Pivot are genuine reusable finite-graph mechanisms. Their strongest demonstrated leverage is *conditional on already having exact targets or constituent basins*. Resource-safe target construction transfers as a guarded schema, but target invention remains largely human/predeclared. Repeated lookup-like floors and failed transfer results show that discovery has not been compressed uniformly.

### C. Proof/certificate compression
G7/G8 demonstrate real proof-DAG amplification and sparse certificate payloads; G14 adds preservation and immutable dependency identity. These results improve representation and replay after truth is available. G8's F09/F10 economics show that compact payloads do not imply cheaper verification, and none of these results establishes cheap discovery.

### D. Global connectivity
No result through G14 certifies a nontrivial route from the standard initial position into the solved islands, and no all-Black-replies root certificate exists. The first strategic open edge is not 'make the proof store better'; it is to obtain a scalable lifting/discovery mechanism and then a START_REACHABLE all-reply connector.
