**`STOP_CURRENT_METHOD`**

### Core reason

Across R0–R5, the programme demonstrates substantial exact restricted mathematics, reusable finite-game operators, exact shallow START_REACHABLE generation and local universal-Black certification, but it never demonstrates the load-bearing operation required for the global theorem: **material, economical, repeatable certified progress connecting start-reachable high-material chess to a compatible broad certified basin network**. - **R0:** restricted exact truth, composition and infrastructure are real, but scalable lifting, middlegame connectivity, start-to-basin connectivity and all-reply closure are separate open edges.
- **R1 — PARTIAL:** exact restricted material-signature solving scales and the inherited target transfers, but H2 breaks the frozen strategy-language hostile threshold and unexplained residual truth does not systematically shrink.

- **R3 — PARTIAL:** exact shallow START_REACHABLE generation with every Black reply explicit and strong semantic White branching compression is practical, but retained White choices are not certified non-losing and no compatible basin is reached.
- **R4 — FAILED:** Bidirectional Certified Frontier is coherent, but its first exact start-side mechanism certifies **0/1,024 D and 0/1,024 H**; existing lower exact targets are too distant and the unrestricted bounded baseline exceeds 2 GiB.
- **R5 — FAILED:** FERL-4 proves exact high-material nonterminal targets are possible, but coverage is **0.48828125% D / 0.68359375% H**; unrestricted White gives the same counts; cost ratios **0.8084090244 / 0.8358657001** miss <=0.75; history burden rises to **2.50730178796x / 2.34487182889x**; no bridge dependency is retired and no repeatable successful growth mechanism is demonstrated.

### Why CONTINUE_CURRENT_ROADMAP is rejected

G15–G42 contain sensible planned research stages but no R0–R5 evidence establishes the required bridge mechanism. Infrastructure maturity, remaining stages and local exact scale do not make the sequential roadmap a credible demonstrated route from restricted truth to the standard initial position.

### Why REBOOT is rejected

Bidirectional Certified Frontier remains logically coherent and its bounded falsification mechanics are scientifically strong. But a reboot verdict requires a **demonstrated** bridge operation, not a better process. R4 and R5 do not produce material certified-surface growth, favorable enough economics, dependency retirement or evidence of repeatability. Continuing it would require another speculative target/mechanism search.

### Why INCONCLUSIVE is rejected

Logical uncertainty about unknown future chess methods remains, but the frozen criterion is narrower: R6 must name a single smallest, materially different, evidence-supported discriminator. The R0–R5 record identifies none. “Try another exact target” or “use more compute” would be a new research programme, not a justified discriminator.

### What remains valid

### What is stopped or authorized

The **current method is closed** as a route to the global weak-solution objective.

- G15–G42 are **not authorized** and must not resume automatically.
- Bidirectional Certified Frontier is **not authorized as a reboot**.
- No R7 is authorized.
- No open-ended new target-family search is authorized under this method.

- Future research may restart only after a materially different affirmative bridge mechanism satisfies `REVIEW/CURRENT_METHOD_CLOSEOUT.md` and is explicitly authorized as a new method or reopened review.

The verdict does **not** claim that chess is impossible to weakly solve, that surviving mathematics is wrong, or that a future unrelated method cannot succeed.
