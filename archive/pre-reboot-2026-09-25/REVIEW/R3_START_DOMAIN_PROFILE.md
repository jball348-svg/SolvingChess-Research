# R3 Start Domain Profile

**Session:** R3 — Start-Reachable All-Reply Closure Audit

## Exact semantic state

R3 uses G10.RULES.v1.0 semantics carried forward through G12.STATE.SERIAL.v2:

`STATE = (board64, turn, castling_rights, effective_ep, halfmove_clock, repetition_count_map)`.

Repetition-position identity is board + turn + castling + effective EP. Full-state equality additionally includes halfmove and the repetition-count map.

The standard initial root is:

- normal initial board;
- White to move;
- KQkq castling rights;
- effective EP = null;
- halfmove = 0;
- repetition map = {root REP key: 1}.

Every R3 state is generated by an explicit legal path from that root. No arena-admissible state is inserted by static construction.

## Transition semantics used

The audit-local generator implements:

- ordinary legal moves and captures;
- check legality / king safety;
- promotion/underpromotion;
- castling and permanent rights loss;
- en-passant and effective-EP canonicalization;
- halfmove update;
- exact repetition barrier/count update;
- mate/stalemate;
- automatic fivefold/75-move terminals.

At the frozen five-ply horizon the maximum raw repetition count observed is 2 and maximum halfmove clock is 4, so claim/automatic draw thresholds do not activate. Their state fields are nevertheless retained in equality.

## Prospectively frozen branch domains

The full root remains the source state. R3 then tests two White first-move semantic families:

- **D / KING_PAWN:** any legal White move of the e-pawn from e2, giving e2-e3 or e2-e4.
- **H / QUEEN_PAWN:** held out; any legal White move of the d-pawn from d2, giving d2-d3 or d2-d4.

After the first move, Black is never pruned.

## Important certification boundary

This semantic class defines a **candidate White policy language**, not a non-loss theorem. R3 can certify that selected moves are legal and start-reachable and that all Black replies are represented. Without a typed exact target/basin at the horizon, it cannot certify that at least one retained White move is non-losing.

Accordingly R3 distinguishes:

- exact START_REACHABLE structural states;
- expanded all-reply Black nodes;
- unresolved horizon frontier;
- exact basin entries (none in this run);
- certified non-loss proof states (not established).
