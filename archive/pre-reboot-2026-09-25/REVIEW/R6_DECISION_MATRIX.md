### REBOOT

Supported. The method retains substantial valid assets, but its strongest supported bridge mechanisms repeatedly fail to produce material, economical, repeatable start-to-basin progress, and no materially different evidence-supported candidate remains in the closed R0–R5 record.

Rejected. The record is not exhaustive over all possible future chess methods, but no single smallest materially different evidence-supported discriminator remains. Another target search would be a new speculative programme.
