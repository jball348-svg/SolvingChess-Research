# R4 Reboot Survival Hypothesis — prospective freeze

**Session:** R4 — Reboot Candidate Construction and Survival Attack  
**Freeze status:** PROSPECTIVE — frozen before decisive R4 target-membership/minimax inspection  
**Primary architecture:** Bidirectional Certified Frontier Reboot  
**Result vocabulary:** `SURVIVED`, `FAILED`, `PARTIAL`, `NO_CANDIDATE`

## 1. Primary falsifiable proposition

R4 tests:

> There exists a prospectively defined typed-target certification mechanism that can take a semantically compressed R3-style START_REACHABLE White-policy frontier and certify a nontrivial fraction of its White choices as exact non-loss without first acquiring near-complete outcome truth for the surrounding local game graph.

R4 tests semantic proof compression, not merely semantic branching compression.

## 2. Exact state model

Use G10.RULES.v1.0 semantics with G12.STATE.SERIAL.v2 full theorem-state identity:

- board;
- side to move;
- persistent castling rights;
- effective en-passant;
- exact halfmove clock;
- bounded repetition-count map.

No board-only merging is allowed.

## 3. START_REACHABLE source domains

All states are generated by explicit legal play from the standard initial state.

### D — discovery family

First White move: the frozen R3 king-pawn semantic family, e2-e3 or e2-e4.

### H — held-out hostile family

First White move: **KNIGHT_FIRST**, any legal move by a White knight from its original b1/g1 home square.

H stresses early minor-piece development rather than another central-pawn continuation. It is not used to choose the target grammar, policy, depth rule or thresholds.

## 4. Frozen White strategy program

A maximum-two-phase R3-derived semantic program is used.

### QUIET phase

`ANSWER_CHECK OR CENTER_PAWN`

This is exactly the R3 selected class.

### Switch trigger

`FORCING_MOVE_AVAILABLE`: at least one legal White move is a capture or gives check.

At the first White decision node where this trigger is true, switch irreversibly to TACTICAL.

### TACTICAL phase

`ANSWER_CHECK OR CAPTURE OR GIVES_CHECK`

This is a six-literal total program when the trigger is counted as one semantic literal. There are no square IDs, named openings, move-sequence exceptions or state-ID patches.

At Black nodes every legal reply is retained.

## 5. Frontier construction and tested cohort

Generate the policy DAG from the standard root through **ply 6**, carrying exact theorem-state identity.

The tested White-policy domain is the exact White-to-move ply-6 frontier.

For each branch D/H, create a deterministic prospectively defined cohort of at most 1,024 exact full states:

- up to 512 states with `FORCING_MOVE_AVAILABLE = true`;
- up to 512 with `FORCING_MOVE_AVAILABLE = false`;
- within each stratum choose the lexicographically smallest canonical full-state byte identities;
- if a stratum has fewer than 512 states, take all of it;
- no replacement from the other stratum.

Cohort selection uses no outcome truth and no engine score.

## 6. Frozen typed target grammar

A state may be a direct R4 exact non-loss target only if it satisfies one of:

1. **WHITE_WIN_TERMINAL** — Black to move, no legal moves, Black in check;
2. **DRAW_TERMINAL_OR_CLAIM** — exact stalemate, automatic draw, or a draw claim legally available to the side entitled to claim under the frozen rules;
3. **G12_COMPATIBLE_EXACT_TARGET** — exact full-state-compatible entry into a frozen G12 KQK/KRK/KPK/DEAD object;
4. **RECURSIVE_R4_CERTIFIED** — certified by the same R4 existential-White / universal-Black recursion into targets 1–3.

Material advantage, engine evaluation, horizon survival, opening knowledge and “looks equal” are forbidden targets.

## 7. Exact certification rule

From a tested White state:

- White may use only moves admitted by the frozen current program phase;
- a White node certifies if at least one admitted legal move reaches a direct target or recursively certified state;
- a Black node certifies only if **every** legal Black reply reaches a direct target or recursively certified state;
- exact transposition merging is allowed only on full theorem-state identity;
- horizon exhaustion is `UNRESOLVED`, never draw/non-loss.

This produces exact non-loss/win/draw certificates whenever it returns certified.

## 8. Bounded certification depth selection

Candidate remaining-ply budgets are **1, 3, 5** plies from each tested White state.

On D only, evaluate them in that order using the frozen exact target recursion and select the smallest budget achieving at least 25% certified-policy coverage. If none reaches 25%, freeze depth 5 and record the coverage failure.

The selected depth is then applied unchanged to H.

This rule uses only exact membership in the frozen typed target recursion, not complete local W/D/L.

## 9. Raw local-truth baseline

After the R4 policy object and selected depth are frozen, compute a competent **complete bounded target-reachability baseline** on the identical D/H cohorts and depth:

- White may use every legal move;
- Black remains universal;
- targets are exactly the same typed targets;
- exact full-state identity is unchanged;
- horizon exhaustion remains unresolved.

This baseline acquires complete exact truth for the bounded proposition “can White force one of the frozen exact non-loss targets within the selected local depth?” It is not promoted to complete-chess W/D/L.

## 10. Metrics

`LOCAL_TRUTH_LEAKAGE = complete/local WDL-labelled states used during target/policy selection / complete local states in the validation domain`

Planned value: **0**.

`CERTIFIED_POLICY_COVERAGE = exact certified White-policy cohort states / tested White-policy cohort states`

`CERTIFIED_BLACK_CLOSURE = represented legal Black replies in certified objects / all legal Black replies from certified Black nodes`

`CERTIFICATION_RESIDUAL = tested cohort states not certified / tested cohort states`

`CERTIFICATION_COST_RATIO = prospective policy certification work / complete bounded all-White target-truth baseline work`

Report both wall time and unique state/edge work; wall time is primary only when implementation paths are comparable.

`PROOF_LEVERAGE = complete baseline unique states / unique states explicitly carried by the union of accepted policy proof objects`

`PROOF_PAYLOAD_RATIO = accepted proof-object unique states / complete baseline unique states`.

## 12. Pass/fail thresholds

A strong `SURVIVED` classification requires all:

- coherent architecture frozen before decisive test;
- exact START_REACHABLE full-state cohort;
- White program prospectively frozen;
- exact Black reply closure 100%;
- state identity violations 0;
- omitted/illegal Black replies 0;
- no engine/evaluation pruning;
- no opening-line memorisation;
- no board-only merging;
- at least one nontrivial semantic target/class certified prospectively;
- D certified-policy coverage >=25%;
- H held-out certified-policy coverage >=15%;
- held-out residual degradation <=2x D residual;
- program <=2 phases and <=6 semantic literals;
- LOCAL_TRUTH_LEAKAGE <=5%;
- CERTIFICATION_COST_RATIO <=0.75 for strong SURVIVED;
- PROOF_PAYLOAD_RATIO <=25%;
- no absolute move-sequence exception;
- no state/square patch list;
- complete within frozen budget.

If exact certification exists but economics, held-out transfer, payload or generality fail materially, classify `PARTIAL`.

If certification requires essentially the complete bounded baseline first, or the mechanism produces no meaningful exact certification and no materially different bridge object, classify `FAILED`.

If no coherent exact mechanism can be stated, classify `NO_CANDIDATE`.

## 13. Strike conditions

A strike is warranted if R4:

- merely renames G35–G39;
- tests only another solved island;
- reports branching compression without exact non-loss certification;
- consumes essentially complete local truth and calls it discovery;
- does not retire or sharply narrow the exact-certification dependency.

A failed frozen experiment may avoid a strike if it strongly discriminates the reboot architecture and materially sharpens R5/R6.
