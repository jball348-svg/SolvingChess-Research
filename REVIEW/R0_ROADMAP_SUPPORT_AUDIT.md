# R0 Roadmap Support Audit

**Scope:** G15–G42 in `roadmap/normalized/G9_G10_Onwards_Long_Range_Plan.md`  
**Question applied to every stage:** *What already-established theorem, empirical scaling law, or mechanism makes this stage more than a description of the unsolved work that must eventually be done?*

## Classification rule

- **EVIDENCE_BACKED_AT_RESTRICTED_SCOPE** — there is a prior mechanism that genuinely performs the same kind of operation, but only on narrower domains than the roadmap stage requires.
- **ENGINEERING_PREREQUISITE** — the stage is technically well motivated and may be necessary, but success would chiefly improve reliability/scale/storage rather than close a chess-logic edge.
- **ASPIRATIONAL_RESEARCH** — the stage names a scientifically meaningful target, experiment or milestone, but no existing theorem or scaling law presently implies that the central transition will work.

These labels are not a viability verdict. They identify how much prior mechanism exists before the future work begins.

## Era-level synthesis

### G15–G21 — foundation and scale

This era is the best-supported part of the future roadmap. G8 proved that a major G7 scale boundary was implementation-specific rather than fundamental; G10–G14 created much stronger state, replay, distribution and storage contracts. That makes additional scale experiments rational.

What is **not** established is a general state-growth or discovery-cost law. The one prospectively frozen eight-man re-ascent is evidence of capability, not evidence that repeated nine-/ten-man ascent with relaxed restrictions will continue to behave similarly.

### G22–G28 — basin map and automated discovery

This era contains the first major support gap. G22 can catalogue truth already obtained, and G26–G28 have credible engineering antecedents. But G23–G25 are precisely capabilities the G9 assessment said the programme did not possess: automatic target discovery, automatic useful strategy-language discovery and automated theorem/abstraction synthesis.

The roadmap does not cite an established mechanism showing that automation will find high-leverage exact objects rather than rediscovering lookup-like local distinctions.

### G29–G34 — middlegame bridge

This is predominantly aspirational. G32 has a genuine restricted precedent: capture-signature DAGs plus Branch/Hyperkernel reuse exact lower endpoints. But no current result demonstrates that this composition remains discovery-efficient in 10–16+ piece domains with ordinary middlegame king safety, quiet strategy and dense interactions.

G33 is especially important: "build a certified middlegame basin network broad enough for opening search" is a statement of the desired missing object. It is not supported by a theorem that such a network can be generated at tractable cost.

### G35–G40 — start-position connector and closure

G35 has strong state-semantics engineering antecedents, but G36–G40 have no direct empirical precedent at the required scope.

- G36's meet-in-the-middle connector is a search strategy; it does not guarantee a useful intersection.
- G37 can reuse filtered-attractor semantics only after a start-relevant target exists.
- G38's all-reply condition is mathematically well-defined by the attractor recurrence, but the economics of closing the Black-reply frontier are unknown.
- G39/G40 are milestones/deliverables, not mechanisms.

The roadmap is candid about this: failure to find G36 intersections sends the programme back to G29–G34.

### G41–G42 — reproduction and preservation

These are credible engineering endgames if a proof exists. They cannot help establish the missing proof route.

## R0 answer to the roadmap question

The future roadmap contains a mixture of strong engineering prerequisites, a few restricted-scope mechanisms worth testing at larger scale, and several stages that are currently descriptions of the unsolved bridge/connectivity work.

The most consequential unsupported transitions are:

1. **G17 / G22:** turn one restricted eight-man ascent into a repeatable scaling/lifting law;
2. **G23–G25:** turn human/predeclared discovery into automated high-leverage truth discovery;
3. **G29–G33:** create a broad middlegame network rather than another restricted island;
4. **G36–G38:** connect the exact start state to that network and close every opponent reply without near-enumerating the relevant game graph.

R0 therefore preserves the roadmap as a useful experiment ordering, but not as evidence that those transitions are already mechanistically solved.
