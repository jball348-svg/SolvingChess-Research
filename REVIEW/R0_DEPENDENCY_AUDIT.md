# R0 Dependency Audit

**Session:** R0 — Claim and Provenance Reconstruction  
**Purpose:** reconstruct the logical dependency route from the standard initial position to machine-checkable White non-loss without using G-numbers as logical edges.

## Executive dependency finding

The repository contains three real but separate assets:

1. exact truth in small full-rule anchors and larger **restricted** historical arenas;
2. reusable finite-game composition/factorization machinery; and
3. increasingly capable solve, certificate, replay and storage engineering.

Those assets do **not** currently form a connected proof route from the standard initial position.

The first genuinely open strategic edge is:

> **restricted exact truth + finite-graph composition → scalable truth discovery/lifting across less restricted higher material**

Nothing in G6–G8 establishes a repeated-ascent theorem or empirical law showing that useful exact targets/basins can continue to be *discovered* with favorable economics as restrictions are removed. Branch/Hyperkernel and Filter-Pivot are real mechanisms, but they consume already-defined targets/basins. G8 shows that one prior engineering frontier was removable and reaches two prospectively selected restricted eight-man islands; it does not establish the global lifting edge.

On the start side, a second independent open front begins after the full-rule state model: the repository has no nontrivial machine-checkable START_REACHABLE all-reply proof domain rooted at the initial position.

## Required transitions highlighted by R0

### 1. Restricted exact endgame truth → reusable higher-tree truth

**Status: ESTABLISHED_ONLY_AT_RESTRICTED_SCOPE.**

G6/G7/G8 show genuine reuse: exact lower-material endpoints can be typed as targets; Branch/Hyperkernel can compose alternatives; Filter-Pivot can factor strategy-language exits; G8 material signatures recover and re-ascend to restricted eight-man truth. This is stronger than a single tablebase.

The restriction is load-bearing. The eight-man R01/R02 arenas fix multiple pawns on brink squares and vary a narrow bishop-colour relation. There is no demonstrated sequence showing the same economics after repeatedly relaxing those restrictions.

### 2. Reusable higher-tree truth → broad middlegame structure

**Status: OPEN.**

No current theorem or empirical scaling law bridges from restricted endgame-like arenas to ordinary middlegame topology. G29–G34 name sensible research domains—transition-rich material, king safety, quiet strategy, exchange networks—but they are the work to be done, not evidence that the bridge already exists.

### 3. Middlegame basins → START_REACHABLE states

**Status: OPEN.**

G10 makes the reachability type distinction explicit. G11 reachable-game fuzz proves that the state machinery can be exercised from the initial position, not that a strategically useful region is certified. G35/G36 plan a forward canonicalizer and meet-in-the-middle search; no certified intersection presently exists.

### 4. START_REACHABLE states → all-opponent-reply closure

**Status: OPEN.**

The finite-game existential/universal predecessor rule is established mathematically, but there is no evidence that the unresolved Black-reply frontier near the opening can be clustered/compressed enough to avoid essentially enumerating the relevant game tree. G38 is a research stage intended to discover whether this can be done.

### 5. All-reply closure → initial-position certificate

**Status: OPEN, but logically straightforward once closure exists.**

The certificate assembly problem is not the principal mathematical uncertainty. The hard dependency is obtaining a start-connected, all-reply-closed body of certified truth first.

## Parallel supporting dependency: trusted verification and storage

G8, G12, G13 and G14 materially improve solver/verifier/certificate/store engineering. They are necessary infrastructure for a credible final proof, but they are not substitutes for N05/N07/N09/N10.

The open G12-through-G13 mandatory replay is therefore classified as a **routing/acceptance blocker**, not the Chess Bridge Problem. Closing it would unlock planned infrastructure/scaling work under the old roadmap, but would not establish a new chess proposition or connect a solved island to the initial position.

## Four-way separation required by R0

### A. Solved-state scale
- Pilot/G3–G7: exact restricted finite arenas of increasing size and topology.
- G8: recovered six/seven-man truth and R01/R02 at roughly 82.5–88.4 million dependency states.
- G12: much smaller but full-rule reference anchors.

**Inference allowed:** the stack can solve and replay some nontrivial finite arenas.  
**Inference forbidden:** these counts measure distance to the standard initial position.

### B. Discovery leverage
- Strong: strict attractor closure from exact targets; Branch/Hyperkernel over known alternative basins; Filter-Pivot factorization; some guarded target-schema transfer.
- Weak/open: automatic target discovery, automatic useful strategy-language discovery, repeated ascent without hand-designed restrictions, ordinary middlegame abstraction.

**Key distinction:** deriving a large closure from a small kernel after the constituent truth is known is not the same as discovering that constituent truth cheaply.

### C. Proof/certificate compression
- G7/G8 demonstrate sparse seeds and product certificates.
- G14 gives immutable typed storage and dependency identity.
- G8 negative controls show verifier cost can exceed producer cost despite compact payloads.

**Key distinction:** small certificate size is not evidence of small discovery cost.

### D. Global connectivity
- No initial-position ancestor appears in a chess proof object.
- No START_REACHABLE entry edge into the historical solved islands is certified.
- No complete all-Black-replies closure exists.

**Key distinction:** large restricted state counts are not evidence of proximity to the standard initial position.

## Exact stopping points

The current global route stops at **E04/E05**: scalable lifting/discovery. Even if that were solved, E08, E10/E11 and E13 remain independent research obligations.

The current start-side route stops at **E09**: a certified nontrivial START_REACHABLE domain has not yet been built.

R0 therefore does not interpret the roadmap as one nearly completed chain. It is a set of well-developed local components separated by several genuinely open edges.

## R0 conclusion

No dependency contradiction was found that forces an immediate verdict, but the logical graph is substantially more discontinuous than a sequential G-number narrative suggests. R1 should test whether G7→G8/R01/R02 contains a real scaling law or only a successful restricted engineering ascent.
