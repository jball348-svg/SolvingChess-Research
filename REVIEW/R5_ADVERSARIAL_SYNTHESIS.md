- exact full-rule theorem-state identity;
- bounded START_REACHABLE forward generation;
- 100% explicit Black reply representation in the tested start-side mechanisms;

FERL-4 is a real nonterminal exact intermediate target at **30–32 pieces**.

Its exactness:
- does not use engine evaluation;
- does not use complete W/D/L;
- does not restate “non-losing”;

- survives unchanged on the knight-first hostile family;
- has zero observed target-exactness violations;

This answers an important R4 uncertainty: exact high-material proof-relevant targets are not conceptually impossible.

### The review mechanics are scientifically productive

R3, R4 and R5 all fail or partially fail cleanly without threshold repair. The reboot-style bounded experiment discipline is materially better than the old G-count progression as a way of learning whether a bridge exists.

Those facts are the strongest evidence R6 could cite if it concludes that some narrowly bounded reboot remains justified.

## 2. Limits of the positive case

The positive case does **not** contain a demonstrated material progress mechanism.

FERL-4:
- certifies only **0.4883% D / 0.6836% H**;
- has the same root counts under unrestricted White;
- misses the <=0.75 economic gate;
- requires a much broader start policy whose exact frontier is millions of states;

The positive case is therefore an existence proof for one exact target class, not evidence that the current method can traverse chess.

## 3. Strongest negative case

The negative case is cumulative, not dependent on one bad experiment.

### Discovery/lifting remains the first global break

R0 found that the dependency graph is discontinuous. Large restricted exact islands and mature proof machinery do not themselves provide a scalable truth-discovery/lifting mechanism.

R1 showed bounded exact producer scaling but hostile strategy-language transfer failed.

R2 showed excellent prospective semantic discovery on a six-piece conversion-rich domain, yet discovery cost **1.278x** complete truth and relied on very large exact lower dependencies.

No repeated favorable higher-material ascent has been demonstrated.

### Start-side compression repeatedly fails to become proof progress

R3 produced impressive structural branching compression while retaining all Black replies. But retained White choices were not proved non-losing.

R4 then attempted the most direct certification route. Terminal/draw/current G12 targets certified **0 / 1,024 D and 0 / 1,024 H**.

R5 supplies the strongest remaining escape hatch: a genuinely exact high-material nonterminal target. It is real, but certifies only **5 / 1,024 D and 7 / 1,024 H**.

Thus three successive start-side steps show:

1. semantic branching can be compressed;
2. distant existing exact targets do not certify it;
3. a new exact local target can exist but remains far too sparse to materialize the compressed frontier.

### Exact history is adverse, not disappearing

R3: roughly 1.79x / 1.73x full-state/board multiplication at ply 5.

R4: 1.35x / 1.48x at its narrower ply-6 policy.

R5 broader search: **2.51x / 2.34x**, with as many as **264 / 217** exact theorem states sharing one board.

The method cannot safely recover that cost through board-only transpositions.

### Economics do not provide a rescue

- R2 discovery loses to solving at 1.278x.
- R4 unrestricted bounded truth exhausts 2 GiB while cheap policy certification proves nothing.
- R5 has nonempty exact certification, but only at **0.808x / 0.836x** baseline wall cost while covering under 0.7%.

The recurring problem is not merely raw speed. It is absence of an object that is simultaneously:
- exact;
- dense/material;
- transferable;
- cheap to discover/certify;
- compositional toward the global theorem.

### The lower/start semantic distance remains enormous

Current trusted lower targets are predominantly tiny-material exact dependencies. R4 proved those are unreachable in its shallow 30–32-piece local tests.

R5 constructs a high-material target but does not show that it creates a connected chain toward lower certified truth. The start and lower surfaces therefore remain separated.
