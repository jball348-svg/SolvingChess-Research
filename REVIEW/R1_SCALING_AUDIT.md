## 1. Precise mechanism tested

R1 tested the conjunction of:

1. material-signature decomposition with exact reuse of lower material;
2. an unchanged G5 semantic target plus strict target attraction;
3. a prospectively frozen G8-style semantic move-language palette and one-switch filtered attraction;
4. separate rank/Bellman verification.

The mechanism was tested across two fixed-pawn material ascents, one same-material restriction relaxation, one wider-band hostile control and one higher-mobility/checking hostile control.

## 2. What survives

### Exact truth production

Strongly survives at R1 scope.

Across 1.63M–29.73M dependency states:

- every final producer completes well inside budget;
- every state passes the separate rank/Bellman verifier;
- normalized producer cost remains roughly 0.16–0.25 seconds per million dependency states;
- the main P0–P3 descriptive log-log runtime exponent is ≈1.09;
- material-signature decomposition keeps the largest active signature materially below total dependency truth.

This is genuine evidence that the material-signature engineering idea is repeatable on controlled restriction relaxation, not merely a one-off G8 recovery.

### Semantic target transfer

Also survives strongly.

The unchanged G5 target has zero false positives in all six R1 arenas and retains substantial win-basin coverage:

- roughly 32–34% throughout P0–P3;
- 29.6% on wider-band H1;
- 74.0% on rook hostile H2.

This is a real prospectively held transfer result.

## 3. What does not survive

### Fixed strategy-language transfer

H2 falsifies the hostile transfer gate.

The full frozen palette is allowed, and R1 selects the best ordered pair after exact evaluation, but only 43.504% of the ordinary strict target basin is reached. The preregistered hostile threshold was 75%.

This is not a failure of FILTERED_ATTRACTOR or FILTER_PIVOT mathematics. It is a failure of the supplied chess strategy vocabulary to remain useful under the hostile topology.

### Increasing proof share

The fixed target does not explain an increasing fraction of full truth through P0–P3. The unexplained exact-win residual stays around 66–68% and reaches 70.4% on H1.

Thus R1 finds **stable transfer**, not a compounding discovery-compression law.

## 4. Engineering scale versus discovery leverage

R1 makes the separation sharper than R0:

- **Engineering scale:** positive.
- **Target reuse:** positive within the tested family.
- **Strategy-language reuse:** positive in the bishop family, negative on H2.
- **Automatic target/language discovery:** still open.
- **Residual raw truth:** stable/mixed, not demonstrably shrinking.
- **Relevance to unrestricted chess:** only modestly improved.

Faster exact solving is therefore not being used as a substitute for scalable truth discovery.

## 5. Restriction relaxation and chess relevance

The main ladder genuinely relaxes pawn geometry at P2→P3 and H1. H2 genuinely increases checking/mobility topology.

However, all new R1 arenas still have a bare-king opponent and historical board-only rules. They do not yet test the mutually interacting capture/exchange topology of ordinary middlegames.

R1 therefore provides a credible local scaling law for the exact engine but not a bridge law to unrestricted chess.

## 6. Verifier economics

Within the new R1 harness, verifier economics improve/stay controlled:

- P0 V/P 0.871×;
- P1 0.763×;
- P2 0.693×;
- P3 0.680×;
- H1 0.663×;
- H2 0.636×.

Peak verifier RSS remains below producer RSS.

Historical G8 F09/F10 reversals still stand, so the correct global conclusion is **topology-dependent**, not “verification is always cheaper.”

## 7. Scaling law verdict

A defensible bounded statement exists:

> In the R1 one-sided, board-only rank-band family, exact producer/verifier cost scales approximately with dependency-state count over the tested range, and material-signature decomposition retains a working-set advantage.

No defensible law yet exists for:

- full-rule history state;
- opposing mobile material;
- dense exchange networks;
- middlegame topology;
- start-reachable closure;
- automatic abstraction discovery.

R1 therefore does **not** extrapolate its ≈1.09 descriptive exponent to chess.

## 8. Status of R0's first open dependency

R0 identified `RESTRICTED_EXACT_TRUTH_TO_SCALABLE_LIFTING_DISCOVERY` as the first genuinely open strategic dependency.

R1 **narrows but does not close** it.

The exact-compute half is materially stronger after R1: controlled ascent/restriction relaxation is repeatable in this bounded family.

The discovery half remains open because:

- the unexplained residual does not shrink;
- no automatic target/language discovery exists;
- H2 prospectively breaks the frozen strategy palette;

The open dependency should therefore be refined to:
