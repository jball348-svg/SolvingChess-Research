# R2 Historical Discovery Reconstruction

**Session:** R2 — Topology-Adaptive Proof Discovery Audit

## Controlling distinction

R2 distinguishes five activities that the historical programme sometimes placed close together:

1. **human semantic invention** — choosing chess concepts/atoms;
2. **candidate generation** — enumerating concrete predicates or move languages from a fixed grammar;
3. **candidate selection** — ranking those candidates, potentially using outcome truth;
4. **post-hoc interpretation** — inspecting exact counterexamples and naming a compact family afterwards;
5. **prospective exact validation** — freezing an object, then testing exactness/coverage on unseen truth.

Universal finite-graph operations such as strict attractor, Branch/Hyperkernel and Filter-Pivot are not themselves target/language discovery algorithms.

## G5 — prospective grammar, outcome-informed object selection

G5 was an important methodological advance because arena restrictions, operation inventory, semantic vocabulary, critical-slab width and lookup stopping convention were frozen before new arena outcome inspection. Its frozen atoms included promotion/interception clocks, support/contact, relative coordinates, exact lower truth, mobility/access and blockade-conversion semantics.

However, the final common target was not generated outcome-blind. The first frozen support/contact candidate was falsified by draws, and a template sweep then selected the largest zero-counterexample target in the frozen vocabulary:

- pawn d6/d7;
- WK within four king steps of d8;
- BK at least five king steps from d8.

Thus G5 cleanly demonstrated **prospective language transfer plus exact outcome-selected target selection**, not autonomous zero-label target invention.

The local tail was then separated with the frozen DSL. Exact trees reached 15 leaves / 25 knight geometries and 46 / 87 bishop geometries, crossing the predeclared lookup-scale stopping convention. This is first-class evidence that a rich fixed language can still encounter description floors.

Source: `handoffs/normalized/G5_Prospective_Transfer_Benchmark_Technical_Handoff.md`.

## G6 — reusable algebra, guards mainly discovered through exhaustive counterexample structure

G6 promoted strict attractor, Branch-Kernel and finite-n Hyperkernel into reusable graph operations. These are genuine exact theorem machinery once targets/basins are supplied.

G6 also produced valuable chess-semantic objects:

- FAR5/FAR3 guarded restoration;
- the exact 96-state relative-coordinate knight exception family;
- RESOURCE_SAFE_TARGET;
- topology-typed critical-manifold and local-grammar regimes.

But the restoration domains and compact exception family were obtained by exhaustive scans/counterexample analysis, and every RESOURCE_SAFE_TARGET instantiation still required zero-counterexample certification in its declared arena. The schema is reusable; a general target-discovery algorithm was not established.

Branch/Hyperkernel can amplify a small kernel after constituent basins exist (for example 4,302 bishop c-file kernel states generating 117,430 pure bridge states), but that is **composition leverage**, not evidence that constituent semantic basins are cheaply discovered.

Source: `handoffs/normalized/G6_Final_Technical_Handoff.md`.

## G7 — prospective arena mining and a new universal operator, but hand-supplied strategy semantics

G7 froze higher-tree candidate selection using unlabeled structural quantities such as scale, branching, transition diversity, checking density, symmetry and topology novelty. That is genuinely outcome-independent **arena selection**.

The strongest new abstraction, FILTER_PIVOT, is graph-general and survived 100,000 arbitrary graph regressions, 24/24 G6 backward-fire arenas and higher-tree tests. This is a real theorem discovery.

But CHECK was a human-supplied first strategy language. Its usefulness was learned from exact truth:

- F01 retention 99.9836%;
- F02 95.0721%;
- F09 29.6787%.

G7 correctly separated the universal Filter-Pivot theorem from the topology-dependent CHECK instantiation. It did not supply an automatic generator/ranker for strategy semantics.

G7's restoration/sanctuary refinements also followed exact resource/counterexample inspection. The session did not claim autonomous predicate invention.

Source: `handoffs/normalized/G7_Higher_Tree_Position_Discovery_Technical_Handoff.md`.

## G8 — prospectively frozen human palette, exact selection after solve

G8 froze a six-language palette before outcome inspection:

`CHECK, CAPTURE, FORCING, KING_ONLY, PAWN_ONLY, MOBILE_PIECE_ONLY`.

No post-outcome predicate was added. Ordered one-switch evaluation then found near-complete coverage on many recovered brink/seven-man families, but only 55.498% on F09.

This is strong **prospective transfer of a hand-authored meta-language** plus exact outcome evaluation. It is not automatic strategy-language invention.

G8 also showed compact certificate payloads, but certificate size and discovery cost were explicitly separate. F09/F10 were verifier-economic negatives.

Source: `handoffs/normalized/G8_Certificate_Engine_and_Reascent_Final_Technical_Handoff.md`.

## R1 — fixed-object transfer deliberately leaves invention open

R1 reused the unchanged G5 target and a fixed G8-style palette. This gave a clean transfer/scaling test. Exact truth production and target transfer survived, but hostile rook H2 reduced best one-switch coverage to 43.504% versus the frozen 75% threshold.

R1 therefore sharpened the unresolved dependency from general exact scaling to topology-adaptive proof discovery.

Sources: `REVIEW/R1_SCALING_RESULTS.md`, `REVIEW/R1_SCALING_AUDIT.md`.

## What G23/G24/G25 were supposed to automate

The frozen G9 roadmap explicitly reserves:

- **G23** for automatic semantic target discovery from typed atoms with train/held-out discipline and exact proof;
- **G24** for automatic strategy-language discovery from move semantics such as threats, simplification, king safety, pawn breaks and piece improvement;
- **G25** for automatic guarded theorem synthesis from counterexample structure plus subsumption/backward-fire validation.

Those were planned capabilities, not established assets. R2 therefore tests a bounded precursor of G23/G24 directly rather than assuming the roadmap stage names represent solved mechanisms.

Source: `roadmap/normalized/G9_G10_Onwards_Long_Range_Plan.md`.

## Historical conclusion controlling R2

Before R2, the repository supported:

- prospective semantic vocabularies;
- outcome-informed exact candidate selection;
- post-hoc compact counterexample families;
- universal graph composition/factorization once semantic inputs are supplied;
- exact held-out validation of hand-frozen targets/languages.

It did **not** support:

- a demonstrated zero-label top-domain target miner;
- a demonstrated semantic move-language miner;
- evidence that such discovery is cheaper than exact truth;
- exact held-out topology transfer of an automatically selected target/program.

R2 is designed to discriminate exactly those missing claims.
