# R2 Negative Evidence

**Session:** R2 — Topology-Adaptive Proof Discovery Audit

## 1. Discovery is not cheaper than solving

The principal R2 negative is preregistered and decisive.

Accepted D1 discovery wall:

**33.9099 s.**

Accepted complete exact-truth acquisition:

**26.5349 s.**

`DISCOVERY_COST_RATIO = 1.2780 > 1.0`.

A later replay narrows the gap toward parity but does not establish a robust sub-solve advantage. The economic gate therefore fails and R2 cannot be `SURVIVED`.

## 2. Zero top-label leakage does not mean zero prior truth

R2 consumes no complete D1 top-signature W/D/L labels before object freeze, so the prospectively defined truth-leakage ratio is exactly zero.

But the discovery mechanism does solve and consume:

**18,293,044 exact D1 lower-signature states.**

The selected target's strongest literal is explicitly:

`WHITE_CERTIFIED_LOWER_CAPTURE_EXIT_EXISTS`.

Thus R2's positive result is best described as **automatic discovery over a trusted exact lower-material dependency layer**, not abstraction from chess structure alone.

This is legitimate under the frozen proposition and compatible with the programme architecture, but it is a load-bearing assumption for scaling.

## 3. The discovered target is conversion-dominated

The selected exact target is:

`LOWER_WINNING_CAPTURE_EXIT OR (WHITE_TO_MOVE AND WHITE_PROMOTION_LEGAL)`.

That is compact, exact and automatically selected, but it is close to the conversion boundary of a brink-pawn arena. Its huge attractor says that most White wins in D1/H1 can force either immediate promotion or certified favorable simplification.

It does **not** show automatic discovery of:

- long-horizon king-safety structure;
- quiet positional improvement;
- broad pawn-break plans;
- dense multi-piece tactical motifs;
- ordinary middlegame basin semantics.

## 4. Held-out transfer is strong but narrow

H1 is a real structural hostile test: changing the Black bishop colour complex sharply changes direct mobile-piece capture traffic and capture-destination balance.

The target/program transfer survives almost perfectly.

However, D1 and H1 share:

- the same material signature;
- the same fixed f7/d2 brink pawns;
- the same board-only rules;
- the same promotion-dominated resource structure.

Therefore exact transfer across this pair is not evidence of transfer across piece types, central pawn structures or middlegame topology.

## 5. Residual raw truth improves dramatically, but not universally

D1 residual outside the composite proof object:

**0.0473208%.**

H1:

**0.108067%.**

This is dramatically better than R1's ~67% main-ladder residual.

The improvement is real at R2 scope, but it is tightly coupled to the discovered conversion target and brink topology. R2 records no universal “residual shrinks with material” law.

## 6. Strategy success does not falsify the historical negative controls

The frozen R2 program `GIVES_CHECK -> ATTACKS_BLACK_PAWN_AFTER_MOVE` reaches 99.7451% in D1 and 99.5773% unchanged in H1.

Historical F09 and R1-H2 fixed-palette failures remain valid. R2 changes the conclusion from “fixed palettes fail” to “a bounded semantic meta-language can adapt on this pair.” It does not prove one universal language.

## 7. Arena remains far from full chess

Both R2 arenas:

- contain six pieces;
- fix both pawns one move from promotion;
- omit repetition/halfmove/castling/en-passant state;
- are ARENA_ADMISSIBLE only;
- have no certified start-position ancestor;
- contain no broad exchange network beyond two bishops and two pawns.

No full-chess or START_REACHABLE inference is permitted.

The first implementation attempt spent excessive time in a generic `staticValid` overlap-sort loop and did not complete the discovery pass inside the execution window.

Before any discovery object or top W/D/L was produced, the implementation was changed to logically equivalent direct pairwise occupancy checks. No arena, predicate, threshold, candidate order or scientific budget changed.

Accepted source/executable hashes:

- executable: `6c8dfad8eca6df10f6c35e07b1008b557d1576c84240cb46a309cc38d8420aef`.

This is an engineering correction, not positive chess evidence.

## 9. R2 negative synthesis

R2 falsifies the strongest economic version of the discovery proposition at this scope:

> useful exact proof objects can be discovered without top outcome labels and can transfer, but the tested discovery procedure is not cheaper than simply acquiring the exact truth it is meant to replace.

The open strategic risk therefore shifts from “can discovery exist at all?” toward:

- whether discovery can become economically favorable;
- whether it remains exact when conversion-dominated brink structure is removed;
- whether dependence on large exact lower-material layers remains manageable.
