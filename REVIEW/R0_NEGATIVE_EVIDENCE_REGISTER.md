## 2. Failed universalisations and transfer failures

- **Pilot 2 autonomous-entry hypothesis failed on the hard frontier.** Only 27.4% of winning White-king placements in the 254 strategic geometries could force the autonomous-pawn target.
- **G4 enemy-pawn restoration failed strongly.** Adding the enemy pawn destroyed many projected wins; only guarded friendly restoration survived in that arena.
- **G5 unconditional friendly-minor restoration failed.** 1,086 knight and 1,557 bishop restoration failures; most were not immediate stalemates.
- **G5 pure union-BRIDGE transfer failed.** Exact pure bridge increment was zero in both prospective minor-piece arenas.
- **G6 critical-manifold universality failed.** Two-sided race clocks became weakly selective and double-brink clocks became tautological.
- **G7/G8 fixed strategy-language dominance failed.** CHECK retention ranges from near-total in some arenas to 29.68% in G7 F09; G8's best frozen one-switch palette reaches only 55.498% in F09.
- **Sanctuary lifting failed when a new resource gained access.** G7 F10 already had 3,578 immediate mate counterexamples; G8 full F10 truth shows 572,681 White wins among 586,893 BK=h8 states.

These failures narrow the reusable core to typed graph operations plus domain-checked chess predicates; they directly oppose any assumption that a successful local chess rule transfers automatically.

## 3. Discovery leverage versus certificate leverage

- Branch/Hyperkernel can amplify a small exact kernel into a much larger union-only region **after** constituent attractors exist.
- Filter-Pivot can compactly factor the part of an ordinary target basin requiring moves outside a supplied strategy language **after** the target basin and language are fixed.
- G8 can encode some derived proof objects in very small sparse payloads.
- None of those facts by itself reduces the cost of discovering the exact constituent truth, target definitions or useful strategy languages.

**Negative inference preserved:** small certificate size is not evidence of small discovery cost.

## 4. Verification-economic negatives

G8 explicitly falsifies a universal "verification is cheaper than solving" story:

- F09: verifier about 16.08 s versus producer 9.74 s, approximately 1.651× slower.
- F10: verifier about 14.08 s versus producer 9.77 s, approximately 1.441× slower.
- F01/F02 proof verification wins wall time but peaks around 0.55–0.62 GB because reverse graphs are materialized.

The exact ratios are implementation/hardware specific, but the qualitative negative is robust: certificate bytes and verifier compute/memory are separate scaling variables.

## 5. Historical provenance breaks

### G4
G6 clean-room reconstruction matches the 1,356,280-state universe and embedded Pilot-2 regression but **does not** reproduce G4's full W/B/D split. The surviving discrepancy is isolated to capture/lower-material graph details and the original implementation-level graph is absent.

R0 classification: the historical G4 report remains evidence of what the session claimed, but its load-bearing full-table truth is **DEPENDENCY_BROKEN** for independent review until source/certificate evidence exists. Later G6/G7 claims should not use the word "authoritative" as a substitute for replay.

### G5 rank label
G6 reconstructs G5 load-bearing membership/count claims but not the frozen knight maximum-rank label under the later canonical rank convention. This is preserved as a certificate-format/provenance exception rather than a W/D truth break.

### G10/G11 original artifact chain
The original G10 byte/vector payload was unavailable during G11/G12, so historical G11 remains HOLD against the original gate. Later state reports that bytes have been recovered, but the original replay still has not been performed.

## 6. Rejected and superseded computations

The repository contains several useful examples where attractive results were rejected after implementation faults:

- G5 provisional bishop truth: superseded by corrected W/D counts and tail sizes.
- G6 missing reverse predecessor for rank-2 double pawn push: the apparent restoration "phase transition" was superseded; early knight FAR5 edge failures were also superseded.
- G7 slider successor defect, lazy Hyperkernel base population, missing lower dependency in fortress post-analysis, and duplicate-predecessor issue: affected outputs discarded/rerun.
- G7 F03/F04/F06/F07/F08 incomplete retrogrades: no partial W/D truth accepted.

This record is a strength of the scientific discipline, but it also means later audit must consume the corrected/superseding artifact, not the most optimistic earlier narrative.

## 7. Rules-model non-transfer

G6's wrong-colour fortress counterfactual changes **274,296 exact outcomes** when promotion is treated as immediate White win rather than faithful continuation.

G12 then demonstrates identical board placements whose outcome changes under repetition/halfmove history.

Therefore:

- old board-only or shortcut-terminal truth is not automatically full-rule truth;
- an entry from a full-rule start-connected proof into a historical table requires exact state/model compatibility;
- broad historical state counts cannot simply be pooled into one final tablebase.

## 8. ARENA_ADMISSIBLE versus START_REACHABLE

No historical Pilot–G8 arena proves that its states are reachable from the normal start. G10 explicitly creates separate types to prevent this conflation.

G11's reachable-game fuzz is useful engineering evidence that metadata transitions can be propagated from the root, but it is sampled and contains no proof strategy/all-reply closure.

G12 seeded history controls are deliberately arena-admissible and explicitly do not claim every seeded history is start reachable.

**Negative inference preserved:** no current large exact chess island has a certified START_REACHABLE ancestor path from the initial position.

## 9. Synthetic engineering is not chess truth

G13's 65,536-state reference campaign is an exact synthetic acyclic minimax workload. It is excellent evidence for deterministic checkpoint/repartition/CAS behavior, but contributes zero chess positions.

G14's strongest proof-store campaigns consume that synthetic workload and store metadata. G14 explicitly adds no chess truth.

This prevents infrastructure progress from being counted as progress along the chess dependency edge.

## 10. Open acceptance blocker with limited strategic information

The mandatory G12-through-G13 distributed replay remains unperformed. It blocks old-roadmap routing to G15/G17, but R0 finds no basis for treating its closure as evidence about the Chess Bridge Problem.

Closing it could show that the distributed platform faithfully reproduces known small full-rule truth. It cannot show that the system can discover or connect new broad chess truth.

## 11. Negative evidence summary for R1+

The recurring risk pattern is not "the code never works." Much of the code works well at declared scope.

The recurring risk is:

> exact restricted truth is obtainable; proof objects can often compress or compose it; but the final difficult frontier repeatedly becomes topology-specific, lookup-like, or dependent on targets that were already supplied.

R1 should therefore measure whether G7→G8→R01/R02 contains a genuine repeated-ascent scaling signal before any review gives strategic credit to the future G16/G17 ladder.
