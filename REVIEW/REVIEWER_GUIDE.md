# Independent reviewer guide

Treat the repository as evidence to challenge, not a sequence of achievements to ratify.

For each material claim:

- restate it without G-number shorthand;
- list exact domain, restrictions, state identity and rule semantics;
- classify it as exact, formal, independently replayed, empirical, engineering-only, conditional, superseded, failed, blocked or not claimed;
- check that verifier scope equals downstream consumed scope;
- distinguish arena admissibility from start reachability;
- distinguish certificate/representation compression from reduction in truth-discovery cost;
- inspect negative controls and rejected runs;
- check whether universal-looking abstractions have hypotheses that become rare or expensive in ordinary middlegames;
- measure verifier cost separately from producer cost;
- trace every initial-position edge to a mechanism stronger than "search until it meets a basin".

## Bridge-first rule

Whenever continued research is proposed, state a candidate Chess Bridge Theorem or scaling proposition precisely enough to falsify. Then try to break it first.

Prefer prospectively chosen hostile domains, counterexamples, lower-bound arguments and scaling extrapolations over additional friendly examples.

## No score laundering

Do not change a programme percentage unless a named obligation was retired or a named dependency failed. Percentages are summaries, not evidence.

## Preserve useful failure

Killing the current route while preserving valid finite results is a successful audit outcome.

Every review session appends to `LEDGER.md`. Corrections are new entries; old entries are not rewritten.
