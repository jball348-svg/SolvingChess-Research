A future restart requires **new affirmative evidence**, not unused compute or renewed interest. Before research execution resumes, all of the following are required:

1. **A materially different bridge mechanism** from R4 terminal/lower-target attraction and R5 FERL-like speculative target search.
2. **A precise theorem/proposition** connecting at least one central open edge: scalable truth lifting, certified middlegame connectivity, or material START_REACHABLE-to-basin closure.
3. **Affirmative prior support** from a theorem, independently verified external result, or bounded pre-existing demonstration; logical possibility alone is insufficient.
4. **Exact full-rule compatibility** or an explicit proven conversion layer, including history.
5. **Universal Black closure** wherever a start-side proof claim is made.
6. **A prospectively frozen bounded qualification test** with competent baseline, hostile unchanged control, hard resource cap and success/failure thresholds.
7. **Material dependency retirement:** success must retire or materially close a named central bridge dependency, not add another exact island.
8. **Evidence of repeatability:** a reasoned and testable path to repeated growth rather than a one-off local target.
9. **Explicit user authorization** to reopen REVIEW or begin a new method.

A qualifying future method should receive new governance rather than silently resurrecting G15.
