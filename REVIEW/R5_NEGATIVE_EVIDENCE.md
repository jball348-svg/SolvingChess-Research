# R5 Negative Evidence

**Session:** R5 — Hostile Synthesis and Nonterminal Bridge Attack  
**Frozen candidate:** FORCED_EXACT_REPETITION_LOCK (FERL-4)  
**R5 classification:** **FAILED**

## 1. Exact target exists, but is negligible as a bridge

FERL-4 is a genuine high-material exact nonterminal target. That positive fact does not satisfy the R5 bridge requirement.

Certified coverage after the frozen one-pair attraction is:

- D: **5 / 1,024 = 0.48828125%** versus >=20% required;
- H: **7 / 1,024 = 0.68359375%** versus >=10% required.

The failure is not close to threshold.

## 2. Unrestricted White does not reveal a hidden local basin

The competent same-proposition baseline admits every legal White move at the attraction node.

It certifies exactly:

- D: **5 / 1,024**;
- H: **7 / 1,024**.

Therefore the tiny result is not primarily a defect of the frozen semantic White filter. At the declared local depth, FERL-4 itself is sparse.

## 3. Irreversible replies destroy repetition contracts

The exact attack encountered very large numbers of repetition-barrier branches:

- D policy run: **718,197**;
- H policy run: **1,039,312**.

Chess near the opening contains abundant pawn moves, captures and castling-right-changing resources. A four-ply exact repetition lock is structurally possible but rarely universal over Black.

No exception was added for these branches.

## 4. Favorable economics fail

Wall cost ratios against the exact same-proposition baseline:

- D: **0.8084090244**;
- H: **0.8358657001**.

Frozen strong-survival requirement: <=0.75.

Recorded exact-work proxies are likewise **0.76462 / 0.80015**, not robustly below the gate.

Thus the candidate does not gain enough computational leverage even before accounting for its tiny coverage.

## 5. Compact certificate payload does not imply cheap discovery

Accepted certificates are tiny:

- D: 13 witness edges;
- H: 7.

But discovering them required millions of exact candidate/reply checks.

This repeats the historical warning from G8/R2: proof representation can be compact while truth/target discovery remains expensive.

## 6. Broader policy greatly enlarges the start frontier

R5's prospectively broader White language was intended to give a high-material target a fair chance.

It produced:

- D: **3,903,222** exact ply-6 states versus R4's 158,947;
- H: **3,004,564** versus R4's 449,623.

The exact frontier expanded roughly **24.56x D** and **6.68x H** compared with R4.

The candidate therefore trades semantic freedom for a much larger exact start-side surface without producing material certification.

## 7. History multiplication worsens

R5 ply-6 full-state / board multipliers:

- D: **2.50730178796x**, max 264 theorem states per board;
- H: **2.34487182889x**, max 217.

This is materially more adverse than R4's 1.34595x / 1.48250x and max 15.

No safe board-only quotient is available.

## 8. No named bridge dependency is retired

R5 does establish existence of a nonterminal exact high-material target class.

But the controlling dependency was not merely “does any such target exist?” It required a target capable of certifying a **material fraction** of the start frontier with favorable proof economics/payload.

That requirement remains unfulfilled.

`DEPENDENCY_REDUCTION = NO REVIEW DEPENDENCY RETIRED; FERL-4 AS MATERIAL BRIDGE CANDIDATE FALSIFIED AT FROZEN SCOPE`.

## 9. No repeatable successful progress mechanism is demonstrated

FERL-4 can be tested repeatedly, but R5 does not demonstrate that repeated application grows a certified frontier materially.

A repeatable experiment is not the same as a repeatable proof-progress mechanism.

## 10. R0–R5 cumulative adverse evidence

The negative case now includes all of the following:

- historical G3–G6 lookup/local floors and topology sensitivity;
- no established unrestricted multi-ascent lifting theorem;
- R1 hostile fixed-language transfer failure;

- R4 complete bounded all-White target baseline failure at the 2 GiB budget;

- R5's strongest clean high-material nonterminal target certifying only **0.49% / 0.68%**;

- R5 certification cost ratios failing the <=0.75 gate;
- R5 policy/frontier and history burden expanding sharply.

## 11. No post-hoc rescue

After observing the R5 result, the audit does not:

- enlarge the four-ply cycle;
- add a second target family;
- add named opening moves;
- permit near-repetitions;
- use engine equality/evaluation;
- add square/state exceptions;
- deepen the attraction;
- shrink or resample cohorts;
- change the D/H families;
- weaken coverage/economic thresholds.

Those would invalidate the prospective test.

## 12. Negative conclusion

R5 produces a stronger negative than R4 in one important sense:

> R4 could still say “perhaps the missing object is a high-material exact nonterminal target.” R5 constructs such an object, proves it exact, and shows that its density/economics are nevertheless far too weak to provide material start-side bridge progress.

This does not prove that every conceivable chess proof method is impossible.

It is direct adverse evidence against keeping the **current method** alive merely on the promise that a richer exact intermediate target will rescue it.
